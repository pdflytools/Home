Why Use an Online ID Photo Stamper?

For almost all competitive exams like SSC, UPSC, Railway, and State PSCs, the official recruitment boards mandate candidates to upload recent passport size photographs with their Name and Date of Photo clearly printed at the bottom. Doing this manually in complex photo editing software can ruin the image dimensions, aspect ratio, or resolution.

Guidelines for Perfect Stamping

  • Format & Background: Always use a clear photograph with a light or white background. The text strip should have a high contrast—white background with black text is the universally accepted standard.
  • Maintain Proportions: Do not stretch your image. Our tool automatically attaches a clean strip at the bottom of your photo, preserving your face's true aspect ratio and resolution.
  • Accurate Details: Ensure there are no typos in your Candidate Name. Use the "Set Today" quick button to instantly apply the current date to avoid date format errors.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: ID photographs are sensitive personal data. Unlike traditional online editors, we process all pixel data locally via HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never uploaded to the cloud, ensuring absolute privacy.
