Why Protect Your Images with Watermarks?

Whether you are a photographer, content creator, or business owner, protecting your visual content from unauthorized use is a priority. Image Watermark Pro provides a powerful solution to overlay custom text or logos onto your images instantly.

Watermarking Best Practices

  • Opacity Matters: A watermark shouldn't ruin the viewing experience. Keep opacity between 30% to 60% so your image remains clear while still being protected.
  • Use Pattern Tiling: A single logo in the corner can easily be cropped out. Enable "Pattern Tiling Mode" to cover the entire image diagonally, making theft nearly impossible.
  • Choose the Right Format: Export as JPEG for smaller file sizes or WebP for optimal web performance without losing quality.
  • Client-Side Security: Unlike many online editors, our tool processes all pixel data locally via HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never uploaded to the cloud, ensuring absolute privacy.
