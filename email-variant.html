      What is the Gmail Variant Generator?

      Welcome to PDFly Tools. The Gmail Variant Generator Pro is a powerful utility designed to help you create thousands of valid alternate email addresses (aliases) from a single base Gmail account. Every variant generated here will route emails directly to your primary inbox.

      How The Tricks Work

      • The Dot Trick: Google's Gmail system ignores periods (dots) in the local part of an email address. For example, emails sent to john.doe@gmail.com, j.o.h.n.doe@gmail.com, and johndoe@gmail.com all arrive at the exact same destination.
      • The Plus Trick: Gmail ignores any text that comes after a plus sign (+) in the email address. This means johndoe+newsletter@gmail.com and johndoe+testing@gmail.com are completely valid and will go straight to johndoe@gmail.com.

      Why Do I Need This?

      Using unique variants allows you to set up precise Gmail filters. You can organize incoming emails into specific folders automatically. Furthermore, if you use a unique plus variant for every website you sign up for, you can easily identify which website sold your data if you start receiving spam.
