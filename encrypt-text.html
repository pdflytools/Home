A Secure & Fun Way to Communicate

Welcome to PDFly Tools. The Text Encryptor Pro provides a unique and highly secure way to mask your private conversations. Instead of sending raw text that anyone can read or intercept, this tool converts your messages into a fun, completely random-looking sequence of Unicode emojis.

How PDFly Text Encryptor Works

  • Step 1: Input Data. Enter your private text, attach an optional URL, and create a strong password to lock it.
  • Step 2: Processing. Our system uses a local XOR cipher algorithm in your browser memory to encode the data.
  • Step 3: Emoji Mapping. The encrypted binary is mapped directly to a massive set of 256 standard emojis.
  • Step 4: Decryption. Share the emojis or the direct URL link. The recipient simply pastes it, enters the agreed password, and reveals the data!
