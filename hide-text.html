What is the Premium Ghost Text Generator?

Welcome to PDFly Tools. Our Ghost Text Generator is an advanced steganography and cryptography utility designed to mask sensitive information within completely normal-looking paragraphs. Whether you are passing secret notes, sharing passwords securely, or embedding hidden digital watermarks, this tool ensures absolute invisibility.

How PDFly Ghost Text Works

We combine Unicode manipulation with military-grade encryption to secure your data:

  • AES-256 Encryption: If you set a password, your secret text is heavily encrypted using CryptoJS before hiding.
  • Binary Conversion: The encrypted data is transformed into a binary stream.
  • Zero-Width Injection: We use Unicode characters (U+200B and U+200C) that take up zero pixels on a screen. These invisible characters are woven directly into your 'Cover Text'.
