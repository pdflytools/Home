Best Practices for Conversion

  • Reorder Visually: Once uploaded, you can drag the image thumbnails in the grid to sort them perfectly before converting. Use the buttons on hover to rotate them if they uploaded sideways.
  • Page Formatting: For photos, "Auto-Fit Image" is best so there are no white borders. For standard documents, choose "A4" with a "Small Margin".
  • Use Filters: If you took a photo of a physical document, select the "B&W (Scan)" filter. It will make the background white and the text dark, exactly like a real scanner.
