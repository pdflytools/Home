Why Use Our Photo & Signature Merger?

When applying for competitive exams, government jobs, or academic admissions online, candidates are often required to upload a single image containing both their passport-sized photograph and their signature. Manually aligning and merging these images can be frustrating and may result in rejected applications due to incorrect dimensions or poor visual quality.

Best Practices for Official Documents

  • Crop Accurately: Ensure your passport photo has no distracting background, and your signature is cleanly cropped without excessive blank space around the ink.
  • Proportions Matter: A signature that is too large or too small can lead to form rejection. Use our intuitive 'Signature Scale' slider to keep the signature width between 60% and 80% of the photo width.
  • White Background is Crucial: Most government portals require a solid white background. Our tool automatically detects transparent PNG signatures and applies a flawless white background to prevent dark or unreadable output on the final document.
  • Maintain High Quality: Set the export quality slider above 90% and use JPEG for the best balance of strict file size limits and visual clarity.
