Why Use PDFly QR Code Generator?

Our completely free QR Code Generator empowers businesses, developers, and individuals to create highly engaging, custom-designed QR codes without any subscription fees. Whether you need a simple URL code for a poster, a detailed digital VCard for a networking event, or a complex WiFi login format, our platform processes everything instantly and entirely inside your browser.

The Importance of High-Quality QR Codes

While a standard black-and-white QR code gets the job done, custom-designed QR codes that integrate your brand colors and logo have been proven to significantly increase scan rates. Our tool automatically calculates Error Correction (up to 30%) to ensure your QR code remains highly scannable even with a large logo positioned right in the center.

Uncompromising Security

Unlike other generators that log your URLs and data, PDFly Tools uses pure Client-Side processing. Your data, text, and uploaded logos are never sent to our servers. What you create on your device, stays on your device. Once exported, your QR code will never expire and has no scan limits.
