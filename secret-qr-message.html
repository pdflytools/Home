Why Use Encrypted QR Messaging?

Standard QR codes are incredibly useful, but they offer zero security. Anyone with a smartphone camera can point and read whatever text or URL is hidden inside. The Encrypted QR Messenger solves this by scrambling your data into an unreadable cipher before it even becomes a QR code.

How It Works Securely

By leveraging the robust CryptoJS library, your text is locked using an AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) cipher and your custom password. The resulting string of random characters is what gets converted into the QR graphic.

Best Use Cases

  • Private Keys & Wallets: Safely print paper backups of sensitive crypto keys knowing they are password protected.
  • Secret Notes: Pass physical notes or messages that only the intended recipient (who knows the password) can decrypt.
  • Zero-Knowledge Safety: Because the tool operates 100% locally via Javascript, even we (the developers) cannot see or store your passwords or messages.
