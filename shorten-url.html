The Free, Permanent & Secure URL Shortener

URL Shortener Pro by PDFly Tools provides seamless link transformation for digital marketers, content creators, educators, and developers. Long, clunky URLs with complex UTM tracking parameters can look suspicious and break across email or social media feeds. This tool instantly converts them into sleek, trustworthy, and actionable short links.

Key Benefits of URL Shortener Pro:

  • Universal Device Compatibility: Shortened links work immediately across iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux.
  • Custom Aliases: Replace random hashes with friendly names like tinyurl.com/pdfly-sale.
  • Built-in QR Generator: Instantly download scannable barcodes for flyers, presentations, resumes, and business cards.
  • Multi-Engine Redundancy: Connects to multiple global shortening providers so link creation never fails.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is strictly protected. At PDFly Tools, we do not log, track, or sell the links you shorten.

  • URL shortening requests are forwarded securely to certified public shortening engines (TinyURL, is.gd).
  • Your link history is saved locally in your browser's storage, not on our servers.
  • We do not inject ads into your shortened links.

Terms of Service
