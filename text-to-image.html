Tips for Perfect Prompts

  • Be Specific: Instead of "a dog," write "A golden retriever puppy wearing a red collar sitting in a sunny green park."
  • Use Enhancement Tags: Click our quick-add tags like "8K Detail" or "Cinematic" to instantly improve the lighting, rendering quality, and texture of your output.
  • Utilize Negative Prompts: Tell the AI what not to draw. Adding words like "blurry, deformed, low resolution, watermark" to the negative prompt ensures a much cleaner, professional result.
  • Experiment with Styles: A simple prompt can look entirely different depending on the style. Try changing your output from "Photorealistic" to "Cyberpunk" or "Anime Studio" for completely fresh aesthetics.
