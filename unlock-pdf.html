Why Use an Online PDF Unlocker?

It's frustrating when you need to quickly edit, print, or copy text from a PDF you own, but you are blocked by a forgotten permission password or an annoying open password. PDFly Tools allows you to easily strip those restrictions away permanently without needing premium desktop software.

Best Practices for Unlocking PDFs

  • Known Passwords Only: This tool is designed to remove the password permanently from documents where you already know the password. It is not a brute-force hacking tool.
  • Select Output Quality: Higher quality settings preserve image and text crispness but will result in a larger output file size.
  • Client-Side Security: Bank statements and pay slips contain sensitive info. Since our tool runs entirely in your browser, your data is never sent to a cloud server.
