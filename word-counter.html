Why Use PDFly Word Counter Pro?

Whether you are writing an essay, crafting the perfect tweet, or optimizing an SEO meta description, knowing your exact text metrics is crucial. Word Counter Pro is a free, blazing-fast, and comprehensive text analysis tool designed for professionals, students, and content creators.

Beyond Simple Counting

Most word counters simply split text by spaces. Our advanced algorithm digs deeper. It separates standard characters from numbers, identifies special symbols, and specifically tracks the usage of Unicode Emojis. It also calculates accurate sentence and paragraph structures.

Reading & Speaking Time Estimates

Preparing for a speech or a presentation? Our tool automatically estimates how long it will take to read your text silently (based on an average of 200 words per minute) or to speak it out loud (based on 130 words per minute). This is an essential feature for podcasters, public speakers, and video creators.
